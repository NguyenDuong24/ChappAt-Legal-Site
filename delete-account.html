Account and Data Deletion

Last updated: May 29, 2026

This public page allows users of SaiGon Match (package name: saigonmatch.com.vn) to request deletion of their app account and associated personal data without needing to sign in to the app.

Important Verification: To protect your account security, we must verify that the requester controls the registered email or account before deleting any personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

Please email our support team directly from the email address connected to your SaiGon Match account:

Request Deletion via Email

If the button above does not open your email client, please manually send an email to supportsaigonmatch@gmail.com with the subject “SaiGon Match Account Deletion Request”.

What to Include in Your Email

  • Your registered email address (or provider sign-in email).
  • Your display name / username in the app.
  • A clear request statement: "Please permanently delete my SaiGon Match account and data."
  • Optional: Reason for deletion.

Data Deleted or Anonymized

Upon verification and processing, the following personal data categories will be permanently deleted or anonymized:

  • Account Profile & Credentials: login records, identifiers, name, profile photo, gender, birth date, bio, education, hometown, and preferences.
  • User Content: social posts, comments, replies, reactions, and private/group chat messages (where technically feasible).
  • Location Data: location history, approximate/precise geohash values, and proximity search logs.
  • Virtual Items & Purchases: balances (Coins/Diamonds/Bánh mì), gifts received/sent, and reward histories.
  • System Identifiers: Expo push notification tokens and Firebase diagnostics metadata.

Data That May Be Retained

We may retain limited information only as permitted or required under applicable regulations:

  • Platform purchase receipt tokens (Google Play Billing), tax, and financial logs for accounting.
  • Account ban logs, moderation flags, and report details to prevent repeat abuse or protect safety.
  • Conversation history visible to the other participant (to preserve their own chat history).
  • Server backups (deleted automatically on routine cycles).

Processing Timeframe

We will verify your request and reply within 7 days. Verified account deletions will be fully processed within 30 days of request receipt.
