Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

SaiGon Match (package name: saigonmatch.com.vn) is a social matching, chat, discovery, group, media sharing, calling, gifting, and community app. This Privacy Policy explains how SaiGon Match and its developer account duongnguyen1263 collect, use, share, protect, retain, and delete user data.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please stop using SaiGon Match. For privacy requests, contact supportsaigonmatch@gmail.com.

1. Data We Collect

Depending on the features you use and permissions you grant, we may collect:

  • Account & Profile Info: name, username, email, provider IDs, profile photo, age/date of birth, gender, bio, education, work, hometown, and relationship preferences.
  • User Content: social posts, comments, replies, reactions, private/group chat messages, photos, voice notes, and virtual gifts.
  • Location Data: precise and approximate GPS coordinates (when you grant permission) used for radar, proximity search, hotspots, and distance display.
  • Background Location Data: when explicitly permitted, location may be collected while the app is closed or not in use to support continuous proximity notifications and nearby matching.
  • Camera & Microphone: photos/videos (for profiles or chat media) and real-time audio/video streams for VoIP call features.
  • Purchases & Virtual Items: purchase tokens (Google Play Billing), diamond/coin balances, and virtual gift transactions.
  • Technical & Ad Data: device model, OS, IP address, crash logs, diagnostics, push notification tokens, and ad-tracking IDs (AdMob/Google Mobile Ads).

2. Why We Use Data

  • To authenticate profiles, maintain secure logins, and manage user accounts.
  • To provide messaging, group chat, media uploads, calls, notifications, and gifting.
  • To run location-based matching, proximity radar, geohash discovery, and distance calculations.
  • To process transactions, manage wallet balances, and validate in-app purchases.
  • To serve personalized or non-personalized ads via Google Mobile Ads.
  • To moderate content, prevent fraud/spam, block offensive actors, and ensure user safety.

3. Prominent Background Location Disclosure

This app collects location data to enable nearby user discovery, radar matching, hotspot discovery, crossing-path style discovery, distance display, and location-based safety features even when the app is closed or not in use.

Background location is completely optional and requires your explicit authorization. You can revoke location access at any time through your device's system settings.

4. Sharing & Service Providers

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with trusted service providers to run the app:

  • Firebase & Google Cloud: for secure authentication, real-time Firestore database, storage, cloud functions, and push notifications.
  • SDK integrations: Google Sign-In, Facebook Login (auth and graph identifiers), Google Play Billing (IAP receipts), and VideoSDK/WebRTC (end-to-end VoIP calling).
  • Google AdMob: to deliver in-app advertisements and reward events.

5. Data Security & Safety Controls

We apply robust technical safeguards, including SSL/TLS encryption for transmissions, database rules, secure backend APIs, and content moderation systems (AI-driven NSFW filters) to protect your information. No system can guarantee 100% security.

6. Data Retention Policy

  • Active accounts: data is kept as long as your profile is active.
  • Deleted accounts: profile data is erased within 30 days of a verified deletion request.
  • Purchase records & Safety logs: limited data may be retained permanently for legal, accounting, tax, or ban enforcement reasons.

7. Account Deletion & Rights

You can request full deletion of your account and personal data at any time. To request deletion, use the in-app deletion button (Settings → Privacy → Account Deletion) or visit our public Account Deletion page. We process verified requests within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

SaiGon Match is strictly restricted to adults. You must be at least 18 years old to register. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If we detect a minor using the app, their profile and data will be permanently deleted immediately.
