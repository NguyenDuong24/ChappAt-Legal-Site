Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

SaiGon Match (package name: saigonmatch.com.vn) is a free social matching, chat, discovery, group, media sharing, calling, and community app. This Privacy Policy explains how SaiGon Match and its developer account duongnguyen1263 collect, use, share, protect, retain, and delete user data.

SaiGon Match is currently a completely free application with no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or paid features. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please stop using SaiGon Match. For privacy requests, contact supportsaigonmatch@gmail.com.

1. Data We Collect

Depending on the features you use and permissions you grant, we may collect:

  • Account & Profile Info: name, username, email, provider IDs (Google/Facebook), profile photo, age/date of birth, gender, bio, education, work, hometown, and relationship preferences.
  • User Content: social posts, comments, replies, reactions, private/group chat messages, photos, voice notes, and virtual gifts.
  • Location Data: precise and approximate GPS coordinates (only when you grant permission) used for radar, proximity search, hotspots, and distance display.
  • Background Location Data: when explicitly permitted, location may be collected while the app is closed or not in use to support continuous proximity notifications and nearby matching.
  • Camera & Microphone: photos/videos (for profiles or chat media) and real-time audio/video streams for VoIP call features.
  • Technical & Ad Data: device model, OS, IP address, crash logs, diagnostics, push notification tokens, and advertising identifiers (Google AdMob).

2. Why We Use Data

  • Authenticate profiles, maintain secure logins, and manage user accounts.
  • Provide messaging, group chat, media uploads, calls, notifications, and social interactions.
  • Run location-based matching, proximity radar, geohash discovery, and distance calculations.
  • Serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Moderate content, prevent fraud/spam, block offensive actors, and ensure user safety.

3. Prominent Background Location Disclosure

This app collects location data to enable nearby user discovery, radar matching, hotspot discovery, crossing-path style discovery, distance display, and location-based safety features even when the app is closed or not in use.

Background location is completely optional and requires your explicit authorization. You can revoke location access at any time through your device's system settings.

4. Sharing & Service Providers

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with trusted service providers to run the app:

  • Firebase & Google Cloud: for secure authentication, real-time Firestore database, storage, cloud functions, and push notifications.
  • SDK integrations: Google Sign-In, Facebook Login (auth and graph identifiers), and VideoSDK/WebRTC (VoIP calling).
  • Google AdMob: to serve in-app advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalization. You can disable personalized ads in your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

5. Third-Party AI Integration & Data Use

SaiGon Match uses a third-party AI service (OpenRouter, powered by Google Gemma) to provide the AI Matchmaker feature. In full compliance with Google Play's User Data policy requirements for third-party AI integrations, we disclose the following:

  • Limited Use: Data sent to the AI Matchmaker — your natural-language search prompt and anonymized preference signals — is used solely to generate match suggestions within the app. It is not used to train external AI models, sold, or transferred to any party beyond what is necessary to operate this feature.
  • Disclosure: When you use the AI Matchmaker, your search query and general profile context (age range preference, interests, and general location area) are transmitted to our backend server, which calls the OpenRouter API. No raw personal identifiers such as your email address, phone number, precise GPS coordinates, or government ID are sent to the AI service.
  • Consent: By using the AI Matchmaker feature, you consent to this limited data processing as described above. Use of this feature is entirely optional. You can stop at any time by not using the AI Matchmaker screen.
  • On-Device AI (NSFW Moderation): SaiGon Match also uses on-device machine learning (NSFW.js / TensorFlow.js) to screen images locally before upload. This processing happens entirely on your device. No image data is transmitted to any external AI service for this moderation purpose.

6. Location Data: Precise vs. Approximate

In compliance with Google Play Data Safety section disclosure requirements, we clarify exactly how each location type is used:

  • Precise Location (GPS / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): Collected when you actively use the proximity radar, "Chạm Sóng" nearby detection, crossing-paths matching, or hotspot features. Precise coordinates are processed in real-time to calculate distances and surface nearby users. Coordinates are stored as a geohash (not raw lat/lon) in Firestore and are not shared with third parties beyond Firebase infrastructure.
  • Approximate Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used as a fallback for city-level discovery and general matching when precise GPS is unavailable or not permitted by you.
  • Background Location (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION): Collected only when you explicitly grant background location permission via your device's system settings. This enables proximity notifications and nearby match alerts while the app is not in the foreground. This permission is fully optional. You can revoke it at any time: Settings → Apps → SaiGon Match → Permissions → Location → Allow only while using the app.

7. Data Security & Safety Controls

We apply robust technical safeguards, including SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmissions, Firestore security rules, secure backend APIs, and AI-driven NSFW content moderation systems to protect your information. No system can guarantee 100% security.

8. Data Retention Policy

  • Active accounts: data is kept as long as your profile is active.
  • Deleted accounts: profile data is permanently erased within 30 days of a verified deletion request.
  • Safety & moderation logs: limited data (account bans, content moderation flags) may be retained permanently for safety enforcement and to prevent repeat abuse.

9. Account Deletion & Your Rights

You can request full deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. To request deletion:

  • In-app: Settings → Privacy → Account Deletion
  • Web form: Visit our Account Deletion page and send us an email

We process verified deletion requests within 30 days. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

10. Children's Privacy

SaiGon Match is strictly restricted to adults. You must be at least 18 years old to register and use this application. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If we detect or are notified that a minor is using the app, their profile and all associated data will be permanently deleted immediately.
